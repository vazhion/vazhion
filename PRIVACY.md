# Privacy

We process account email, authentication data, subscription identifiers, device fingerprints, and optional analytics events you consent to.

We do not sell personal information. Desktop crash dumps are not uploaded. AI features only leave your machine when you opt in and configure a provider.

You may export or delete account data from the portal. EU/EEA/UK requests target 30 days; CCPA 45 days.
